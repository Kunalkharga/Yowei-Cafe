Our story

A neighbourhood kitchen that cooks like it’s feeding family

Tucked into the Evatt shops on Sharwood Crescent, Yowei has quietly become one of Canberra’s most-loved local takeaways — built on scratch-made curry, hand-crumbed pork loaf, and portions that send people home with leftovers.

There’s no shortcut here: the chicken is crumbed fresh, the curry simmers low, and the person answering the phone usually remembers your usual order. It’s the kind of place people drive four streets — or four suburbs — for.
